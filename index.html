How a project runs

Four clear stages. You always know what's happening and what comes next.

  1. 1

    Talk

    A free call or site visit to understand the problem, the people and the budget.

  2. 2

    Plan

    A written scope, timeline and price, with the system design laid out before any work begins.

  3. 3

    Build & install

    We develop and test in short cycles and install hardware on site, showing you progress as we go.

  4. 4

    Support

    Training for your staff, then updates, backups and servicing for as long as you need.
