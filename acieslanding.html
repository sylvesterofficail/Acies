Questions

        

          

            How long does a project take?
            
A website or small system is usually ready in 2 to 4 weeks. Larger platforms take 1 to 3 months. CCTV and solar installations are typically done within a few days once equipment is confirmed.

          

          

            Will the software work when the internet is down?
            
Yes, where it matters. Point of sale, attendance and clinic systems can keep working offline and sync automatically once the connection is back.

          

          

            Can you combine software with CCTV or solar?
            
That's what we're built for. A school can get its portal, cameras and backup power from one team, with one point of contact.

          

          

            Do you support systems after launch?
            
Yes. Maintenance plans cover updates, security fixes, backups, hosting and scheduled servicing of installed hardware.

          

          

            Where do you work?
            
We're based in Ikot Ekpene, Akwa Ibom State. Software projects run anywhere; installations are scheduled across Nigeria.
