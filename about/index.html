About Acies

A technology company built for how Africa actually works.

Acies is Latin for the sharp edge of a blade, and the front line. We started Acies Africa Technologies to put that edge into the hands of hospitals, schools, government offices, firms and families across Nigeria.

Our story

Acies Africa Technologies was founded by software engineering graduates of Topfaith University with a simple idea: an organisation shouldn't need one vendor for software, another for cameras and a third for power, with nobody owning the result when something breaks.

So we built a company that does all of it. Our engineers write the software, our installers fit the hardware, and the same team stays to keep everything running. Today we build management systems, websites, AI tools and automations, and install CCTV and solar inverter systems for clients in health, government, education, real estate, law and many more industries.

We're registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (BN 9165061, 5 January 2026) and work from Abuja, Port Harcourt in Rivers State, and Akwa Ibom State, serving clients across Nigeria.

How we work

We own the whole system

Software, cameras and power from one team. When something needs fixing, there's one number to call and no finger-pointing between contractors.

Built for real conditions

Unreliable internet and power are design requirements, not excuses. Our systems work offline, sync later and stay on through outages.

We stay after launch

Handover is the start, not the end. We train your staff, keep systems updated and come back to service what we install.
